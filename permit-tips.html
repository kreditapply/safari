Permit Tips for Bandhavgarh Safari Booking

If you’re thinking about enjoying a gypsy safari in Bandhavgarh, then let me tell you that you need to take permits for that from the forest department. But, before that, you should know about some things so that you won’t book the wrong one. Here are the permit tips so that you can enjoy a dreamy vacation in the wild:

1. Make an Advance Booking

Bandhavgarh is a wild spot and is also a famous one among tourists. This park has a huge tourism base, and because of that, the permits, or you can say safari slots for this park, get booked way earlier than the actual safari ride. You can make an online booking for the Bandhavgarh Safari Permit 120 days in advance. It’s always a good idea to make a booking as soon as the permit booking opens. Advance booking will allow you to choose the zone and timing according to your preference.

2. Choosing the Safari Zone

To make a booking, you have to choose the safari zone in which you wanna go and see the animals. For that, it is always advised to get help from the website, read about the zone first, and then choose to make a booking. Knowing about the zone will help you to enjoy a safari according to your preference, with sightings inside the park. Also, choose the zone closest to your hotel or resort (if you have pre-booked them). It is advised to book a permit first and then make other bookings

3. Know the Safari Types Before Booking

The Forest Department of Bandhavgarh National Park allows tourists to enjoy a gypsy safari inside it. This safari offers tourists the opportunity to go inside the park on a gypsy and enjoy animal sighting time. These safaris are done in a group of 6 people. Means, 6 seats can be booked, and 2 others are for the guide and the driver. You can get a gypsy safari permit for Bandhavgarh from mpsafaribooks.co.in.

4. Carry the Same ID Used During Booking

Visitors are requested to come with their ID proof whenever they visit the park for a safari ride. It is important for everyone to carry the original government ID that you used while booking the safari permit (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, etc.). The officials or guards from the forest departments always check each and every ID proof before allowing entry. If your ID proof does not match the details that you have given when making the booking, then your safari can get canceled at the last minute. No refund will be given in such cases. So, make the booking by checking all the information at every step, and come with the original ID proof to the safari gate.

5. All Tourist Details Must Be Accurate

Whenever you make a booking for a group safari then you have to enter the details of every passenger separately. These details include full name, Age, Gender, nationality, ID proof and its number. Fill in the details correctly because even one alphabet or number change can lead to the cancellation of the safari ride. Mistakes in spelling, age, or ID number can cause trouble during entry. Also, don’t get confused between the details and passengers.

6. Permits Are Non-Transferable and Non-Refundable

The forest department of Bandhavgarh does not allow any transfer of permits. If you’re unable to come to the park, then you can cancel it, but giving the permit to someone else is not allowed. The details of passengers are written on permits, and only those people can go for a ride. Also, there is no refund if you cancel your permit at the last minute or miss your safari ride. Be sure about your booking and double-check all the information when making a Bandhavgarh Safari Booking.

7. Reach Safari Gate at Least 30 Minutes Early

You should arrive at the safari gate at least 30 to 40 minutes before your scheduled time. This gives you enough time for ID verification and document checks. In that time, a gypsy safari is also allotted to you, and you have to make the payment of additional charges if the forest department has newly added them. Going early before the ride ensures that your safari will start on time and you don’t have to come outside in less time. This will make sure that you’ll enjoy a safari with maximum chances of animal sightings, and there will be no delays.

8. Carry a Printed Copy of Your Permit

If you make an online Bandhavgarh Safari Booking then you may receive your permit by email or SMSreceive your permit by email or SMS. But, coming with a hard copy at the time of the safari is necessary. You can get a printout of the permit and take it with you when going for a ride. Some gates have poor mobile network or no internet, so digital copies might not work. Also, sometimes the officers refuse to trust the permit that is on the phone, so coming with a printout is highly important.
