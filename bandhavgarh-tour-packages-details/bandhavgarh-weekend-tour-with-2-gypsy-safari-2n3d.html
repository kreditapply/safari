 Tour Itinerary 

The first day of this adventurous Bandhavgarh trip is for relaxation. You have to reach the nearest railway or airport, and then you have to come to the pre-booked resort. You can also take our pickup service at an extra charge. After reaching, you have to check into your rooms and then, after refreshing, you can have a delicious lunch. Take your time to enjoy the surroundings in the evening, or you can simply explore the nearby sightseeing areas. Come back to the resort at night and have your dinner. Rest for the day.

The second day of your Bandhavgarh holiday package is going to be a thrilling one. On this day you will go on multiple gypsy safari rides inside  Bandhavgarh. The first safari will take place in the early morning. It will let you explore the morning beauty of this wild park, and you can also listen to birds chirping. You have to get ready for an early morning gypsy safari ride and also you will have your packed breakfast while riding on the safari. After coming back from the safari, you can take a rest in your rooms. Then get ready for another safari that will take place in the evening. Before going on a safari, don't forget to have lunch. The evening safari will give you another chance to spot the Royal Bengal Tigers and other animals. Come to the resort and have dinner after the safari.

Enjoy a nice breakfast and then pack your bags so that you can check out of your resort. Your thrilling and memorable trip ends here.
