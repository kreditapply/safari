Things to Do in Bandhavgarh National Park

Bandhavgarh National Park is not just a place you visit for wildlife sightings. This park gives an experience to the tourists that stays with them forever. You can enjoy thrilling safaris here, and they are the best experience in Bandhagarh, but this park offers a lot more. Here, one can enjoy their time to the fullest and make memories with good photographs and stories. It’s in Madhya Pradesh and offers people to explore the tribal art, enjoy cycling, gain information about the forest, see amazing sunsets, spot rare birds, and do a lot more to spend a perfect vacation. Some of the most amazing things to do in Bandhavgarh are:

1. Enjoy Tribal Art and Local Handicrafts

The buffer and surrounding area of Bandhavgarh is home to the tribal communities like the Baiga and Gond tribes. They are famous for their art heritage, and you should definitely visit the local craft stalls and community centers to see handmade items, tribal paintings, and eco-friendly decor. You can also purchase the famous Gond paintings and take them home as your memory of this wild vacation. Sometimes, resorts or local organizations even organize live art demonstrations or cultural evenings with music and dance performances. You can get information about them from the locals and even participate in them to spend your time in Bandhavgarh.

2. Go on Nature Walks

Gypsy is not the only option to explore the wild Bandhavgarh National Park. If you wanna explore the tiny flora and fauna of the park, then going on a walking trail inside the buffer zones would be best for you. In these, a trained naturalist walks with tourists and helps them to observe small animals, insects, butterflies, and medicinal plants that you usually miss during a gypsy safari ride. The walking safari is especially for feeling the peace and observing the not-so-famous attractions of this park. This safari is best for experiencing the peace and getting an education about the forest. These take place usually during the morning or the late afternoon. Just keep in mind that these walks take place in the safer areas of the park, where there is less animal activity, so sightings of animals are not there. For the animal sighting, going on gypsy safaris is advised.

3. Watch the Sunrise or Sunset

Bandhavgarh National Park is a great spot that is filled with scenic viewpoints. This place is a wild spot, which is also a beautiful one. You can find a lot of scenic viewpoints in and around Bandhavgarh, where you can go and enjoy sunrise and sunset from there. You can even find the watchtowers in the buffer zone of the park. This allows tourists to see the scenic beauty of the area and spend some relaxing time with fresh vibes and bird sounds all around. If you’re someone who enjoys being in nature and wants to observe the real, raw, and amazing vibes of the Jungle, then go on a safari in the buffer zone. Take some time to relax at the watchtower without being in a hurry, and you can have a great time there. It’s one of those dreamy experiences that stays with people more than the sightings of animals.

4. Wildlife Photography

Bandhavgarh National Park is a wildlife spot that allows sightings of tigers, leopards, Indian Bison (Gaur), elephant, Sambar Deer, Spotted Deer, Wild Boars, Sloth Bears, Indian Wolf, Striped Hyena, Indian Fox, Peafowl, Indian Roller, Black Drongo, Lesser Adjutant, Crested Serpent Eagle, etc in their natural habitat. This park is home to more than 47 animals and 250 bird species. You can take some amazing shots of the animals by going on thrilling gypsy safari rides. Just make sure to keep the flashlights off as they can disturb the animals. If you’re into wildlife photography, then going here gives you endless chances to capture tigers, deer, birds, landscapes, and even tribal life. Many professional wildlife photographers even host short photography workshops in Bandhavgarh, especially during peak tiger sighting season. So you can take part in it and learn respectful photography in the wild.

5. Relax in Peace with Forest Views

There are many comfortable and luxurious stays near the park. Whenever you come to Bandhavgarh for a vacation, you can have a nice time here. The resorts and hotels that are made in the area offer people to spend some time in greenery and soothing vibes. You can even find open verandas, reading corners, and private gardens in some of the properties. After the safari, you can go to your accommodation and spend a good time in Bandhavgarh by taking part in outdoor bonfires and star-gazing evenings with local storytelling. To experience a good time in Bandhavgarh, choose a locally themed resort that offers all these facilities.

6. Explore the Area on a Bicycle

You can go to the buffer zones of the park for cycling trails. This happens with the forest guides and allows people to explore nature from close. Through these rides, you can explore the smaller paths of the forest and deeper areas where gypsy safaris cannot go. Through these bicycle trails, you can simply spot the birds and even spot the cute deer grazing in the Jungle. This activity is quite famous in the area and is a refreshing way to experience the rural landscape and spot hidden details of life around the Bandhavgarh National Park. You can even go to the village areas and talk with locals to get knowledge about Bandhavgahr. This activity is best for explorers who like to find new spots.
