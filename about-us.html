About Us

About Us

Introduction:The website mpsafaribooks.co.in is run and managed by Jungle Safari India, a privately-owned travel company based in Delhi, India. Both Jungle Safari India and the website are private entities. We are not owned by the Government, the Forest Department, or any government body, nor do we represent or claim any connection with them.

Jungle Safari India is one of the trusted names among tourists for wildlife tourism services. This company has a passion to help travelers explore the national parks like Bandhavgarh, Kanha, Jim Corbett, Tadoba, Gir, Pench, Kaziranga, Ranthambore, and many more. We provide all services that are required for a nice trip to national parks, like safari bookings, gypsy rides, and hotel reservations. When you book a safari through our site, the official guide charges are included, which ensures that your safari will be safe and informative.

Founder :

The company was founded by Mr. Abhishek Sinha. He established this company because he is a passionate wildlife lover and nature enthusiast. Jungle Safari India helps people get closer to wildlife by going on safari rides inside the national parks. During the ride, people get a thrilling opportunity to spot the wild animals, and they also learn about the importance of conserving them. Mr. Abhishek Sinha also promotes eco-tourism in national parks and even supports activities like preventing illegal hunting, protecting wildlife habitats, etc. For him, wildlife tourism not only offers thrill and adventure, but the safari also inspires people to protect nature and animals.

Our Journey

The company Jungle Safari India was started back in 2020, and from that time, Mr. Abhishek Sinha has been leading it. He believes in providing ethical and eco-friendly tourism services to people. His idea was to offer services to people through which they can explore the beauty of wildlife without disturbing the animals or damaging the environment. Every safari that is arranged by Jungle Safari India remains safe inside the Jungle for both the animals and tourists. We do that by following the rules of the park and the guides, naturalists, and travel experts of our team know the park routes and animal behaviors really well.

The team of Jungle Safari India includes nature enthusiasts, professional guides, travel planners, and naturalists who know the forests and parks inside out. With our services, people can enjoy thrilling vacations to some of India’s top national parks and also go on informative safaris. During safaris, our trained guides share useful knowledge, explain park rules, and guide tourists so they can enjoy their time in the jungle to the fullest. By following the principle of Atithi Devo Bhava, we offer warm hospitality to our guests, because of which we are one of the most trusted safari operators in India. With our offerings, you can enjoy a great time in Bandhavgarh or other national parks of India.

Through our website mpsafaribooks.co.in You can not only book the safaris, but we also offer tourists the opportunity to make bookings for hotels and resorts near the Bandhavgarh National Park. You can even book the tour packages that include both these options and spend a nice holiday there, solo or with your loved ones. You can explore the hotel options and make online bookings of those that come under your budget and preference. We would like to clarify that the hotels that we have included on our site are not owned by Jungle Safari India, but we have tie-ups with them, which allow us to offer them to our guests.
