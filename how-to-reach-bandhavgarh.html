How To Reach Bandhavgarh National Park

Bandhavgarh National Park is a famous attraction that is located in the Umaria district of Madhya Pradesh. This scenic park is well connected to various cities through road, rail, and air.

By Air

The nearest airport to this famous Bandhavgarh National Park is Jabalpur Airport (JLR) which is around 204 km. This airport has a good connection of flights from Delhi and Mumbai. You can find many airplanes from major airports to Jabalpur. Another option is Khajuraho Airport which is around 260 km from the park. These are the domestic airports. Now, if you’re coming from a foreign country then you need to book your flight to Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport which is in Varanasi. The distance from this airport to the park is around 340 km. After reaching your airport, you can find many taxis or cabs to reach the wild Bandhavgarh National Park.

By Train

The nearest railway station to the famous park of Madhya Pradesh, Bandhavgarh National Park is the Umaria which is around 35 km. Another option for which you can book your tickets is the Katni Railway Station. Both of these stations have a good road network with major cities of India such as Delhi, Agra, Jabalpur, etc. After reaching the station, you just need to go out so that you can take a taxi or cab to reach the park.

By Road

No buses are available from Jabalpur to reach the park. However, because of the good condition of the road, you can take private vehicles, taxis or cabs towards the park. The park has a good road network with major cities. The distance between major cities and Bandhavgarh is:

  • Umaria to Bandhavgarh: 22 km
  • Katni to Bandhavgarh: 100 km
  • Satna to Bandhavgarh: 135 km
  • Jabalpur to Bandhavgarh: 170 km
  • Khajuraho to Bandhavgarh: 222 km
