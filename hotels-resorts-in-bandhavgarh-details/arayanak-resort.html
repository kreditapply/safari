Overview

The Arayanak Resort has been in Bandhavgarh since 2013, and since that time, this has become a famous place to stay among wildlife enthusiasts. This is a huge property that covers more than 12 acres. It is located close to Bandhavgarh National Park, around 5 minutes away from the Tala Gate. This beautiful resort offers suites that are designed to reflect the rustic feel of a rural village. The suites have all the amenities that are required for a nice stay experience. It also has a multi-cuisine dining area where guests can eat delicious food, and the dining area has handcrafted furniture from Bastar that adds to the beauty. This resort has a swimming pool, Meditation, and yoga room, and also an amphitheater. This resort features a library, a watch tower, a spa, a butterfly garden, a conference room, and many other amenities, which make it a great stay option.
